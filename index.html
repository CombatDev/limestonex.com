About Limestone Exploration Company

Limestone Exploration Company was founded to help people discover the natural beauty of Eastern Kentucky through safe, memorable outdoor adventures.

From hidden caves and forested creek valleys to scenic paddle routes and adventure travel experiences, our mission is to connect people with wild places while promoting stewardship, exploration, and respect for Kentucky's natural heritage.
